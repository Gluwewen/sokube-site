feature:
  title: "What is a POC?"
  description: |
    A POC is generally carried out in a controlled test environment with limited time and budget. It aims to demonstrate that the proposed Cloud solution meets the organization's needs and is technically and economically viable. \n The POC can include setting up prototypes, running performance tests, scaling tests, and evaluating integration with the organization's existing systems. It can also involve key members of the organization to assess the solution's impact on business processes and operations. \n The outcome of the POC is generally used to determine whether the proposed on-premise or Cloud solution is retained for a larger-scale implementation or dropped in favor of another solution.
