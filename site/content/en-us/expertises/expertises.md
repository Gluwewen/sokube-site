  - question: "How does a DevOps approach improve software quality?"

      By implementing continuous integration and automated testing processes, DevOps makes it possible to identify and fix errors more quickly, and to ensure better software quality.

  - question: "How does a DevOps approach accelerate development and delivery cycles?"
    answer: |
      By automating tasks and optimizing processes, a DevOps approach speeds up software development and delivery cycles. This can reduce time to market and improve the company's responsiveness to market changes.

  - question: "Can a DevOps approach reduce costs?"
    answer: |
      By improving the efficiency of development, operations and security teams, DevOps can help reduce software operating and maintenance costs. It can also reduce costs linked to errors and delays in development.

  - question: "How does a DevOps approach improve collaboration?"
    answer: |
      By fostering collaboration between development, operations and security teams, a DevOps approach can improve communication and cooperation within the company. This can strengthen team trust and motivation and foster a culture of continuous improvement.

  - question: "Does a DevOps/DevSecOps approach strengthen security?"
    answer: |
      By implementing quality and security management processes, the DevOps/DevSecOps method can strengthen the security of the company's software and data. This can reduce the risk of data breaches and service disruptions.
